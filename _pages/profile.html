From models to systems.

I am Kuo Li, an undergraduate student majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tongji University. I am an incoming Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, starting in 2027. I will be advised by Yongwei Wu and Mingxing Zhang in the MADSys lab.
